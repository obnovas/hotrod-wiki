# The Prewar Speed Shops: Bell, Lee's, Orr, and Jim White

Dean Batchelor wrote in *The American Hot Rod*: "First there was George Wight, then Lee Chapel, then Karl Orr, and then everyone got into the act."[^hem] Hemmings' Daniel Strohl adds a fourth name Batchelor left out.

## Bell Auto Parts (1923)
George Wight started a junkyard in Bell, California in **1923**, noticed many incoming Model Ts carried speed equipment, and resold it cleaned up. The building at **3633 Gage Ave.** came in 1928.[^hem] (The Jalopy Journal spells his name "Wright";[^jj] Batchelor and Hemmings use **Wight**.)

Per the Jalopy Journal, hot rodders brought back reports on parts they ran, and Wight kept notes on what worked. When **Cragar** (Harlan Fengler and Crane Gartz's company, which had taken over the Miller-Schofield/Goossen "Valve-in-Head" Model A head) failed in 1932, Wight bought the heads, patterns and tooling after six months of negotiation. He got the price under $100 by 1933 and introduced an "Improved Cragar Head" in 1935.[^jj] ◐

Wight died in 1943. His widow ran the shop until **Roy Richter** bought it in 1945. Richter built it into an early mail-order speed shop and sold it to Chuck Strader in 1978; Strader ran it in Huntington Beach until 1987.[^hem][^jj]

## Lee's Speed Shop (Lee Chapel), c.1929
A c.1950 catalog posted on the H.A.M.B. claims 21 years in business, which implies **1929**. ◐ *This is an inference from a catalog.* Lee's was at 3263 San Fernando Rd., LA (sharing a junkyard), moved in 1933 to 4557 Alhambra Ave., closed in 1937 while Chapel toured with a midget, and reopened in 1939 at 1143 E. 14th St., **Oakland**.[^hem]

## Orr Speed Shop (Karl & Veda Orr), 1940
Opened in 1940 at **11140 Washington Place, Culver City**. It closed sometime in the 1950s and reopened in the 1960s on Sierra Highway in Mint Canyon.[^hem] See [Veda Orr](../people/veda-orr.md). Veda Orr also photographed for *California Timing News* (Feb 1947 photos of the Summers Merc).[^k]

## Jim White's Speed Shop, 1932
Based on Roger Harrell's book: opened in 1932 near San Pedro & 99th in South Central LA, moved mid-1933 to 11006 S. Main, then in 1940 to 10924 S. Main, run with brother Nick Harrell until **1976**.[^hem] ◐
