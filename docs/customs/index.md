# Customs & Kustom Kulture

*The look: chopped, shaved, lowered and painted deep.*

Customizing is its own tradition inside hot rodding. A hot rod asks how fast; a custom asks how it looks. Early customizing was called "restyling," and its goal was a "fake Cord," not a hot Ford. This department is about the style and the people who built it. How the look was publicized and sold (books, kits, shows, magazines) lives in [Media & Shows](../media/index.md).

*Short on time?* The [Quick Answers](../reference/quick-answers.md#cars-customs-and-techniques) give the short version of the big questions about cars, customs and techniques, each with a link to the full story.

- [Hirohata Mercury](hirohata-mercury.md): Barris Kustom's ice-green 1951 Merc, and the argument over how long it took to build.
- [KKOA & the Lead Sled Revival](kkoa-lead-sled-revival.md): Wichita, 1979–83, when a club brought the lead sled back.
- [Washing the Signs (Ozark Drifters, Ark.)](ozark-drifters-ceola-scrapbook-arkansas.md): according to the Ceola family scrapbook, a Fayetteville club that sentenced its own speeder to wash street signs (1960–64).
- [A Family Portrait on the Car: Dave's Dream (Chimayó, N.M.)](daves-dream-chimayo-lowrider.md): a used 1969 Ford LTD, finished by a family and a car club after its builder died, blessed at the Santuario and sent to the Smithsonian in 1992 (⚠ how much was done before 1978; ⚠ the family portrait, doors or fender).
- ["This Is a Rat Rod Show" (Saugerties, N.Y., and Mahtowa, Minn.)](rat-rod-shows-saugerties-mahtowa.md): a builder told "not now, not ever" starts his own show behind his shop, and a Minnesota club with beer-keg gas tanks parks beside a country store and drives to Las Vegas (⚠ Mahtowa's first year).
- ["Scraping the Sky": Slamfest and Scrapin' the Coast (Tampa and Biloxi)](slamfest-scrapin-the-coast-gulf-trucks.md): a mini-truck club's 1991 show in an outlet-mall parking lot and a Biloxi weekend from 2002 or 2003 that took in lifted trucks and ran into a squat-truck law (⚠ Scrapin's first year).
