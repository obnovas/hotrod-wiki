# Places & Scenes

*Where it happened: regional scenes, and the streets, drive-ins and lots where people gathered.*

Hot rodding wasn't only a SoCal export, and it didn't happen only on strips. This department is organized by place, in two groups.

*Short on time?* The [Quick Answers](../reference/quick-answers.md#places-and-cruising) give the short version of the big questions about places, scenes and cruising, each with a link to the full story.

**Regional scenes** are the places that built their own way of doing it, on abandoned airfields, through volunteer councils and with fairground shows.

- [Regional scenes: how they differed from SoCal](regional-scenes.md)
- [Midwest & East Threads](midwest-east.md)
- [The Ohio Gassers](ohio-gassers.md): Cleveland, Dayton, Columbus and Mansfield door-slammers.
- [Memphis & the Mid-South](memphis.md)
- [Texas](texas.md)
- [Pacific Northwest](pacific-northwest.md)
- [Upper Midwest](upper-midwest.md)

**Cruising and hangouts** are the cruise strips, drive-ins and parking-lot meets of about 1940–1990 that we can source, and how each was policed or ended.

- [Cruising overview: the map and the policing scorecard](cruising-overview.md)
- [Woodward Avenue](woodward.md) and [Woodward's Ordinances](woodward-ordinances.md)
- [Van Nuys Boulevard](van-nuys.md), [Bob's Big Boy & the River Road](bobs-big-boy-river-road.md), [Harvey's Broiler](harveys-broiler-downey.md), [Whittier Boulevard](whittier-blvd.md) and [Modesto](modesto.md)
- [Summer Avenue, Memphis](summer-avenue-memphis.md), [Lake Street & University Ave](lake-street-university.md), [Westheimer, Houston](westheimer-houston.md) and [Central Ave, Albuquerque](central-avenue-albuquerque.md)
- [Main Avenue, Twin Falls, Idaho](twin-falls-main-avenue.md): the street the local paper called a "drag strip," and the timing association that offered to pay for a real one (1957–65).
- [Dragging Main](dragging-main.md): the small-town loop in yearbooks and an oral history, from Franklin, N.C., to Hays, Kan. (1950s–1980s).
