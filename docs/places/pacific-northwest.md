# Pacific Northwest: Councils, Woodburn and Fiberglass

*A volunteer council ran the roadster show and the strip*

!!! abstract "Article record"
    **Confidence:** Medium · **Source types:** <span class="hr-badge">Grassroots</span> council/track sites, Wikipedia (◐), company history · **Last verified:** 2026-10-13 (Round 6) · **Open issues:** Woodburn early details only via Wikipedia/track site · [How we rate this](../reference/source-standards.md)

In Portland the clubs ran the show through a volunteer council, not through promoters. That is what set the Northwest apart.

## A council, then a strip

The MHRC was founded in 1954, with Dee Wescott as first president, alongside "community members and elected officials." It runs the **Portland Roadster Show** with volunteers.[^mhrc][^wes]

The council's strip came seven years later. Woodburn opened in **June 1961** as an **eighth-mile** strip operated jointly by the MHRC and the **Northwest Timing Association**. It was lengthened to a quarter mile in 1963, and Jim Livingston bought it in 1972 (Wikipedia; track site).[^wd][^wda]<sup class="hr-q" id="qr-1"><a href="#q-1" title="Single source so far: see Open questions">◐</a></sup> Whether 1963 and 1972 are the years is still open. Wikipedia and the track site may share a source.

Before Woodburn, Portland-area racers used the area of the vanished Vanport site, now Portland International Raceway (Jefferson61).[^pir]<sup class="hr-q" id="qr-2"><a href="#q-2" title="Single source so far: see Open questions">◐</a></sup> Only that page says so.

## Fiberglass and volunteers

The council's first president had another claim on history. [Wescott's](../street-rodding/fiberglass-suppliers.md) of Boring, Oregon (1954), and Cal Automotive's founders, Navy men from Whidbey Island, put the Northwest at the start of the fiberglass street-rod body trade.[^wes]

Shows and strips were run by **civic-minded volunteer councils** with elected officials involved, rather than by promoters or magazines. Rain also meant more emphasis on indoor shows and coachwork. The volunteers ran the show.
