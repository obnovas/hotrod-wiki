# Contribute

*Corrections, sources, topic ideas and feedback are all welcome.*

This wiki is a running investigation, and readers know things we do not: a cousin who raced at the local strip, a club newsletter in a shoebox, a better date. There are three ways to send something. Each opens a short form on GitHub (a free account is needed).

- **Report a correction:** something on a page is wrong, outdated or misattributed.
- **Share a source:** a local paper, club newsletter, obituary, record, marker, photo or oral history we should read.
- **Suggest a topic or send feedback:** a person, place, club or shop to research, an idea for the site, or general feedback.

<p><a class="hr-btn" href="https://github.com/obnovas/hotrod-wiki/issues/new/choose" rel="noopener">Open the forms on GitHub</a></p>

The same buttons appear at the end of every article, prefilled with the page you were reading.

## What happens next

Every message gets the same handling, whether it is a correction, a source or an idea.

1. **Label.** We tag it `correction`, `source-tip` or `suggestion`, and `triage` until it is handled.
2. **Verify.** Nothing changes on a page because someone said so. We look for a citable source, check it against the [source standards](reference/source-standards.md), and read the original ourselves. A reader's memory is welcome, and is treated as a lead and flagged ◐ single-sourced until a second source turns up. Where sources disagree, the page shows both with a ⚠, and the details go in the page's **Open questions & disputed details** section.
3. **Add to the Research Trail.** A good lead that we cannot settle yet goes onto the [Research Trail](research-trail.md) under "Next breadcrumbs", so it is not lost and can be worked in a later round.
4. **Credit.** If your tip is used, we name you (or "a reader") in the Research Trail log for that round and cite your source in the article's footnotes. Tell us in the form how you want to be credited, or that you want to stay anonymous.
5. **Close.** We close the issue with a note saying what we did: fixed, added as a lead, needs another source, or declined and why.

## What we cannot accept

- **Copyrighted images or long excerpts.** Send a link and a description. We only host public-domain or openly licensed images, each credited.
- **Anything that would need to be paywalled or scraped around.** We do not bypass blocked archives or paywalls.
- **Unsourced claims as fact.** They can still be useful leads; we will say so on the page.

Corrections and suggestions about how the site is organized or how it looks are just as welcome. [How the Site Is Organized](taxonomy.md) explains the departments.
