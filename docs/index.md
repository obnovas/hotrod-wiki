The headline stories of American hot rodding have been told plenty. This archive follows the side roads instead: the people, small shops and ideas that built the hobby, from the 1930s dry lakes to the 1980s street machine. We go looking for **lesser-known** threads, first-hand recollections, and family or club sources, and we lean hardest on local news, obituaries, club newsletters and municipal records.

<!-- hr:highlights -->

!!! note "How to read this wiki"
    - Every factual claim carries a footnote with a source URL.
    - **⚠ Conflict** marks places where sources disagree; both versions are shown.
    - **◐ Single-sourced** marks claims found in only one source so far.
    - The teal **Grassroots** badge marks articles built mainly on local or homegrown sources: [what counts and where we found them](reference/grassroots-sources.md).
    - The [Research Trail](research-trail.md) is the running detective's log of leads.
