# Corrections and takedown

If something on this site is wrong, incomplete, unfair or used without permission, we want to hear from you.

Write to [corrections@hotroddinghistory.com](mailto:corrections@hotroddinghistory.com).

We review every message and respond promptly[ / within X business days]. Nothing on a page changes just because someone asked. We check the sources. Where a living person is the subject of a dispute, we err toward taking the disputed material down while we look.

## What to include

For a correction, a missing credit or a concern about how someone is described, please send:

- The page address (the URL).
- The sentence, caption or image you mean, quoted if you can.
- What should change, and why.
- A source, if you have one: a link, or the title, date and page of a newspaper, magazine or book. If you are relying on memory, say so. We treat a memory as a lead until we can cite it.
- How you would like to be named if we use your help, or that you want to stay anonymous.

## Copyright takedown

If you are the owner of a copyright, or you are authorized to act for the owner, and you believe material on this site infringes that copyright, send a notice to the same address (or to the designated agent below, once one is listed). To be complete, the notice should include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed. If there are several, a representative list is enough.
3. Identification of the material you want removed, and enough information for us to find it (the page URL and a description of the passage or file).
4. Your name, mailing address, telephone number and email address.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and that, under penalty of perjury, you are authorized to act for the owner of the exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

Those items follow 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3). A notice that leaves them out may delay our review.

## Counter-notice

If we removed or disabled material you posted or sent us, and you believe that was a mistake or a misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the same address. A complete counter-notice includes:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the material that was removed, and the location (the page URL) where it appeared before it was removed.
3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, address and telephone number, a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the district in which your address is located (or, if you are outside the United States, for any judicial district in which Adventure Equity LLC may be found), and a statement that you will accept service of process from the person who filed the original notice, or that person's agent.

If we receive a complete counter-notice, we may restore the material unless the original complainant tells us they have filed a court action.
