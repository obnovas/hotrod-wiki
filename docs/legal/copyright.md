**Recommended statement (CC BY-SA 4.0):** You may share and adapt the site's original text, as long as you give credit, link to the license, say whether you changed it, and share any adaptations under the same license. The formal terms are the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This does not cover third-party material.

**The other options, in brief.** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 is the same idea with a "non-commercial" limit. That sounds protective, but "commercial" is fuzzy (a club calendar, a book for sale, a museum gift shop), and it blocks reuse on Wikipedia and in many educational projects. All rights reserved keeps the most control and is the current placeholder; the tradeoff is that researchers, clubs and other archives would need permission for ordinary reuse that a public-history site usually wants to allow. **Recommendation: CC BY-SA 4.0** for the site's original text and compilation. It keeps credit and share-alike, stays true to a free archive, and avoids the traps of a non-commercial clause. See LICENSE-recommendation.md for the repository LICENSE split (MIT for code, the chosen license for content).

## Images

Photographs and other images on this site are third-party works, each under its own license: typically public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA. We do not host an image unless it is public domain or openly licensed. Copyrighted pictures are linked, never copied.

Every hosted image is credited next to the image and on the [Image credits](../reference/image-credits.md) page, with the creator, source and license. Reuse of an image has to follow that image's own license, which may be stricter or more specific than the license for our text.

## Quotations

We sometimes quote a short passage from a copyrighted newspaper, magazine or book, with a footnote, to comment on it or to tell the history. Those quotations remain the owner's. If a quote is too long or you are the owner and want it trimmed or removed, write to [corrections@hotroddinghistory.com](mailto:corrections@hotroddinghistory.com).
