# About and editorial rules

Hot Rodding History is a free, source-cited archive of the hometown history of hot rodding: the strips, clubs, shops and builders where the hobby actually lived on a Saturday night. We cover the 1930s to today, with the heaviest focus on the years before 1990.

The archive is published by Adventure Equity LLC. That name appears in the footer and on these legal pages, and nowhere else.

This page is the short public statement of how we work. The day-to-day rules live on [Source Standards](../reference/source-standards.md) and [Editorial Voice](../style-guide.md). [What Makes This Different](../about/what-makes-this-different.md) explains how we fit with other archives.

## Editorial rules

- Every factual claim carries a footnote with a source URL (or a full print citation when there is no URL).
- When accounts disagree, we show both and mark the claim **&#9888;**. We describe the disagreement as being between sources. We do not treat it as anyone's fault.
- A claim that rests on only one source so far is marked **&#9680;** and treated as provisional.
- We would rather leave a gap than invent a date, a quote, a name or a tidy ending.

## Tone

The standard is **neutral, factual and attributed**.

We celebrate the people and shops who built the hobby. We do not judge anyone's character. We do not publish unverified negative claims about a named person or business. Civic history — towns debating a strip, a period ordinance, a closure — can stay when it is documented and told as history, not as a verdict on someone.

A human reviews every page about a living person, or someone who has recently died, before it is published.

## Corrections

If something is wrong, outdated or unfair, write to [corrections@hotroddinghistory.com](mailto:corrections@hotroddinghistory.com). The [Corrections and takedown](corrections.md) page explains what to send.
