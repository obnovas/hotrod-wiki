# Grassroots Sources

A growing catalog of the local and homegrown sources used in this wiki: what counts, which outlets and archives we have used, and what each contributed. New outlets are added each research round. Industry media still appear in the wiki, but they do not earn the Grassroots badge on their own.

## What counts as grassroots
- **Counts:** local newspapers and local obituaries; oral histories and interviews recorded by local historians; club newsletters, club histories and club-run track pages; municipal, county and police records; historical-society pages and talks; historical markers; family accounts and captions; local-history sites and blogs; enthusiast forum recollections (flagged as anonymous or unverified).
- **Does not count by itself:** national magazines, trade press, NHRA or hall-of-fame features, auction copy, encyclopedias. A metro daily such as the *Los Angeles Times* counts when it covers its own region, as it does for Modesto and Inyokern.
- **Why the caution:** local sources are often the only record of small events, but they are usually unsigned, promotional, or retold from memory. Pages say so, and flag single-sourced and conflicting claims.

## Catalog of outlets used so far
Format: outlet (year read) → what it contributed → the page that uses it.

### Local news
- [Great Bend Tribune](https://www.gbtribune.com/sports/local_sports/1955-back-to-where-it-all-began/) (2011 feature) → the club president's account of why the club went to the city, plus the "birthplace" claim we flag as local pride → [Great Bend](great-bend-srca.md).
- [Los Angeles Times, Oct 10, 2005](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-2005-oct-10-me-dragstrip10-story.html) → how Inyokern's volunteers ran the strip, and the FAA threat that ended it → [Inyokern](inyokern-dust-devils.md). The LA Times on [Modesto, Feb 6, 1990](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1990-02-06-mn-195-story.html) → police chief and club-leader quotes on cruising → [Modesto](../cruising/modesto.md).
- [San Bernardino Sun](https://www.sbsun.com/sports/20120418/motor-racing-services-for-two-racing-legends-this-weekend/) (2012 obituary notice) → Bernie Partridge's career with the Dust Devils and the Inyokern strip → [Inyokern](inyokern-dust-devils.md).
- [Times Herald-Record](https://www.recordonline.com/story/news/local/port-jervis/2015/07/30/ed-almquist-motorhead-inventor-pioneer/33784642007/) (Port Jervis, 2015 obituary; read via search excerpt) → the Mason-jar injector and early prices → [Ed Almquist](../people/ed-almquist.md).
- [Burbank Leader](https://www.latimes.com/socal/burbank-leader/news/tn-blr-river-road-a-quick-route-to-memories-for-oldtime-burbank-drag-racers-20140605-story.html) (2014) → Burbank teenagers racing on the River Road in the 1950s, in their own words (Tommy Ivo and others) → [Bob's Big Boy & River Road](../cruising/bobs-big-boy-river-road.md).
- [Weekly Alibi](https://alibi.com/news/cruising-crackdown/) (Albuquerque alt-weekly, 2006) → how Albuquerque's 2005 cruising ordinance was enforced in 2006, with a cruiser's quote → [Central Ave, Albuquerque](../cruising/central-avenue-albuquerque.md).

### Obituaries and funeral-home pages
- [Wendt Funeral Home](https://www.wendtfuneralhome.com/obituaries/robert-j-bartel) (Bob Bartel, 2018) → birthplace, marriage, the 1956 Cordova farm purchase → [Bob Bartel](../people/bob-bartel.md).
- [Pike County Courier](https://www.pikecountycourier.com/milestones/obituaries/edgar-almquist-EWPC20150728150729961) (Almquist, 2015) → the 1947 founding date that conflicts with Hemmings → [Ed Almquist](../people/ed-almquist.md).
- [Engle Racing Cams obituary page](https://doczz.net/doc/1013817/engle-cams---engle-racing-cams) (company's obituary flyer, mirrored) → Jack Engle's 1939 and 1941 dates → [Jack Engle](../people/jack-engle.md).

### Oral history and interviews
- [Modestoview](https://www.modestoview.com/gene-winfield-interview/) (Chris Murphy's interview with Gene Winfield) → the Century Toppers' founding "in late '47," the coop shop, early racing roads → [Century Toppers](modesto-century-toppers.md).
- [Southern Foodways Alliance](https://www.southernfoodways.org/oral-history/summer-avenue-memphis/) (Summer Avenue, Memphis oral history project, 2021) → the avenue's early chain businesses (the first Holiday Inn, 1952) → [Summer Avenue, Memphis](../cruising/summer-avenue-memphis.md).

### Club newsletters, club histories and club-run pages
- [SRCA Dragstrip, "About Us"](https://www.srcadragstrip.com/about-us) (the Sunflower Rod and Custom Association's own page) → the club's timeline, the Chamber of Commerce letter, the 1993 reorganization → [Great Bend](great-bend-srca.md).
- [Gopher State Timing Association history](https://www.gstarod-custom.com/gsta-history) → how Twin Cities cruising led to street races and the clubs' response → [Lake Street & University](../cruising/lake-street-university.md).
- [Memphis Rodders](http://www.memphisrodders.com/) (club history) → the club's 1947 start and a 1952 exhibit photo, both flagged ◐ → [Memphis](../regions/memphis.md).

### Municipal and police records
- [FBI Law Enforcement Bulletin, Dec 1951](https://leb.fbi.gov/file-repository/archives/december-1951.pdf) (Pomona's chief of police writing for other police) → the point system, the Officers Club rooms, the Fontana lease → [Pomona](pomona-choppers-fontana.md).
- [American Society of Planning Officials report no. 74 (1955)](https://www.planning.org/pas/reports/report74.htm) → what a dozen cities were trying, and the end of the Highway Patrol's club program → [City planners, 1955](planners-report-1955.md).
- [Stanislaus County Library, Modesto Cruising Ordinance (1990-03-27)](https://www.stanislauslibrary.org/memories/about_ordinance.shtm) → the ordinance record that conflicts with a historical marker's wording → [Modesto](../cruising/modesto.md).

### Historical markers
- Ohio History Connection marker 47-77, via [Remarkable Ohio](https://remarkableohio.org/marker/47-77-akron-fulton-airport-champions-raceway/) and [HMdb](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=142783) → the text we compare with NHRA's account → [Akron Fulton Airport](../regions/akron-fulton-airport.md).
- HMdb's [Modesto "Graffiti Summer" marker](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=223773) → the claim that cruising was banned in an ordinance "that remains in force today" → [Modesto](../cruising/modesto.md).

### Family accounts
- [Kustomrama, Jack Engle](https://kustomrama.com/wiki/Jack_Engle) (photos and captions courtesy Mark Engle) → the homemade cam-grinder story and "he and Isky both bought their first cams from Ed Winfield" → [Jack Engle](../people/jack-engle.md).
- [JR Thompson's Gasser Reunion page](http://www.jrthompson.net/gasserreunionchuckfinders.html) (includes "Farewell, Chuck Finders" by his son) → Chuck Finders's racing start and shop, told by his son (family account, ◐) → [The Ohio Gassers](../regions/ohio-gassers.md).

### Local history sites, historical societies and blogs
- [houstonfreewaydragstrip.com](https://www.houstonfreewaydragstrip.com/houston-international-speedway-history.htm) → the strip's local chronology and quoted 1959 Chronicle text, with its own claim that Don Gay bought it → [Houston Freeway](../regions/houston-freeway-dragstrip.md).
- [Historical Society of the Upper Mojave Desert](https://www.ridgecrestca.com/news/hsumd-april-meeting-drag-racing-in-old-ridgecrest/article_7bc2f56d-951b-4f93-8b58-eba04720b1bd.html) (2025 talk announcement) → local memories of racing around Ridgecrest, and the claim that Inyokern was the longest-running strip → [Inyokern](inyokern-dust-devils.md).
- [1947project](http://1947project.blogspot.com/2005/07/hot-rod-boys-spend-afternoon-in-court.html) (a blog that posts daily 1947 news) → a July 1947 Compton court item → [Pomona](pomona-choppers-fontana.md).
- [Barrio Boychik](https://barrioboychik.wordpress.com/2015/12/29/the-cruising-culture-of-east-los-angeles/) (local-history blog, 2015) → a dated account of the March 23, 1979 Whittier Boulevard ban → [Whittier Boulevard](../cruising/whittier-blvd.md).

### Forum recollections
- [H.A.M.B., "Lake Street (Mpls, MN) in the 60s"](https://www.jalopyjournal.com/forum/threads/lake-street-mpls-mn-in-the-60s.341268/) → anonymous cruisers' memories → [Lake Street & University](../cruising/lake-street-university.md).
