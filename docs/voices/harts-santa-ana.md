# The Harts of Santa Ana: Pappy, Peggy and Gerald

!!! abstract "Article record"
    **Confidence:** Medium-High for the outline, Medium for the anecdotes · **Source types:** Hart's 2001 National Dragster interview (quoted in obituaries), the obituaries themselves (NHRA, OC Register, LA Times, NYT, AP, Press-Enterprise, Richard Parks), son's 2015 newspaper interview · **Last verified:** 2026-10-13 (Round 7) · **Open issues:** Peggy Hart's own words not yet found · [How we rate this](../reference/source-standards.md)

C.J. "Pappy" Hart (1911–2004) and his wife **Peggy Hart** ran the Santa Ana drags on an Orange County Airport runway from 1950 to 1959. See [Santa Ana](../eras/1950s-santa-ana-drags.md) and [Peggy Hart](../people/peggy-hart.md). The fan site *We Did It For Love* collects his obituaries, and several of them quote him and the people who knew him.[^wd]

## Pappy, in his words

> "There's been drag racing since cars were invented, but I guess they say I invented drag racing because I was the first one to have a commercial strip. There was one in [Goleta, Calif.], but they charged no fee at the time. I saw a need to get people to stop racing on the streets; that was dangerous."
>
> — **C.J. Hart**, 2001 National Dragster interview, as quoted by NHRA.com (Phil Burgess), June 26, 2004.[^wd]

The New York Times obituary noted that this was "a comment seemingly at odds with his widely reported arrest at age 87 for driving 85 in a 55-mph zone."[^wd]

In April 2004, at a museum panel, a moderator asked why he wasn't driving a Rolls if he was so smart. Hart answered: "Because I'm a Ford man." (NYT, July 4, 2004).[^wd]

## Peggy, as others remembered her

> "At that time, it was mainly roadsters and coupes. Peggy was going as fast as any of them, and beating a lot of guys. She was a trendsetter, a pioneer."
>
> — **Steve Gibbs**, NHRA vice president of competition, to the Press-Enterprise (Lyle Spencer), 2004.[^wd] *Secondhand*: Peggy died in 1980, and we have not found her own words.

**From the detective's notebook:** ⚠ The Orange County Register obituary (Robin Hinch) called Peggy "the only woman racer." That is a tribute-piece superlative we cannot verify, and we treat it as a claim only.[^wd]

⚠ **Opening attendance:** the LA Times (Shav Glick) gives about 500 spectators and about 50 cars, and quotes Hart that the final day drew more than 4,000.[^wd] Compare [Santa Ana](../eras/1950s-santa-ana-drags.md).

## Gerald Hart, 2015

> "Races would be held every Sunday except Mother's Day. They tried it once and they wouldn't let that happen again. Their wives wouldn't let them out."
>
> — Attributed to **"Hart"** in the Press-Enterprise, Oct 1, 2015, in a story that interviews son Gerald. *Ambiguous*: the article does not make clear whether this is Gerald or a quote from Pappy.[^pe]

> "He was 88 years old when I made him retire. He was falling asleep on the freeway."
>
> — **Gerald Hart**, Press-Enterprise, 2015.[^pe]

## Dale Armstrong, racer

> "He knew I would race anything to make few bucks. 'Pappy' - that's what everyone called him - always took good care of me. I was treated better at his track than any other."
>
> — **Dale Armstrong**, who raced at Lions under Hart from 1964 to 1979, to the Press-Enterprise, 2004.[^wd]
