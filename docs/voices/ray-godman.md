# Ray Godman and Preston Davis: Memphis in Their Own Words

!!! abstract "Article record"
    **Confidence:** Medium · **Source types:** NHRA interview feature (2014), BangShift obituary (Jan 3, 2018), Memphis Rodders blog (2008) · **Last verified:** 2026-10-13 (Round 7) · **Open issues:** ⚠ Halls start year and Safari month (below); Commercial Appeal 2008 article not yet read · [How we rate this](../reference/source-standards.md)

Raymond Godman (Memphis) ran the **Tennessee Bo-Weevil** cars, co-founded Lakeland Dragway, and served as an NHRA regional adviser. He died in late 2017 or early 2018 (BangShift, Jan 3, 2018). In 2014 NHRA.com interviewed him and his driver **Preston Davis**.[^nh][^bs] See [Memphis & the Mid-South](../places/memphis.md).

## In his words

Godman came to racing from a war:

> "We were fighting the Chinese, each just trying to kill the other. … It was horrible fighting. In a 24-hour period, we lost 91 dead, 771 wounded. When I got shot, I knew it was bad."
>
> — **Ray Godman**, NHRA.com, 2014, on Sept 17, 1951, in Korea, when he was a 23-year-old Marine with Fox Company, Fifth Regiment. *Told 63 years later.*[^nh]

Two years later he found drag racing:

> "I became interested in drag racing in 1953 just as it was starting to come on. I realized I could be more active in the pits in drag racing than in circle-track racing … I got in touch with Wally at Hot Rod to offer my help."
>
> — **Ray Godman**, 2014.[^nh]

The car's name came late one night:

> "Red and I were working on the car in the garage. It was 2 o'clock in the morning, and we'd just finished making a chicken wire and fiberglass mesh nose. … Red, who was as country as you ever saw, he said that it kinda looks like a [cotton] bo-weevil snout. I said, 'What about a Tennessee Bo-Weevil?'"
>
> — **Ray Godman**, on naming the car with driver James "Red" Dyer.[^nh]

And his driver earned Garlits's respect:

> "We used to attend the Gainesville Turkey Trot races every November to have a best-of-three match race against Don Garlits. After Preston beat him on holeshots one time, Garlits pulled me off to the side and asked me, 'Where'd you get him? That guy is good!'"
>
> — **Ray Godman**, 2014.[^nh]

## Preston Davis

Davis, for his part, gave Godman the credit:

> "He's the history of drag racing in Memphis; there ain't no doubt about it. Raymond and I had our ups and downs over the years — it was mostly over money — but he'd get out of that wheelchair and get under that car with me to check the bearings."
>
> — **Preston Davis**, 2014.[^nh]

### More from the notebook

⚠ **Halls start year.** NHRA's 2014 narration says the Rodders began racing at Halls in **1953**, and that they bought Chrondek timers, set up the track for a Sunday of racing, then tore it down and held their club meeting that evening. Memphis Tech's history says the first sanctioned meet was in 1955.[^nh]

⚠ **Drag Safari month.** NHRA says the Safari came "in the summer of 1955, just before the first Nationals in Great Bend." SEMA, citing Hot Rod (Dec 1955), describes a "September weekend." The first Nationals were in September 1955, so the two accounts do not agree.[^nh] <a class="hr-q-back" href="#qr-1" title="Back to the story">↩</a>
