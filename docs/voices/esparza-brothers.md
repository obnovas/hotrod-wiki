# The "Mexican Express" at Lions

*A 1941 Willys truck raced at Lions for seven years, and no records*

!!! abstract "Article record"
    **Confidence:** Medium · **Source types:** newspaper review of a 2022 documentary, with quotes from interviews in the film · **Last verified:** 2026-10-13 (Round 7) · **Open issues:** We have not watched the film; years raced not pinned down ("early 1960s," "seven years") · [How we rate this](../reference/source-standards.md)

> "If the wind was right, we could be at home and hear the announcer."
>
> — **Rick Esparza**, on Lions Drag Strip, 2022.[^lb]

Rick and Fred Esparza built a **1941 Willys truck** into a drag car they called the *Mexican Express* and raced it at **Lions Drag Strip** in Wilmington (1955–72) for seven years. They set no records. That is part of why their story is worth telling: it is about the many racers who never made the record books. Their father, a Navy man, moved the family to West Long Beach in 1958. Danny Miguel's documentary about them was reviewed by the Long Beach Post's *Hi-lo* on Aug 21, 2022.[^lb]

## In their words

Rick said what the racing kept him from:

> "I could have been in jail. … Sooner or later it would have happened."
>
> — **Rick Esparza**, on what drag racing kept him from, 2022.[^lb]

Kenny Youngblood, on a Saturday night at Lions:

> "You never knew what was gonna show up on a Saturday night at Lions. You might see a twin-engine car. You might see them line up side-by-side, you might see a three-engine car, you might see an airplane engine car, you might see turbine jet cars—all kinds of machinery."
>
> — **Kenny Youngblood**, motorsports artist, in the film (2022).[^lb]

## What the film leaves out

The reviewer notes that the film "doesn't dive as deeply into the cultural significance or challenges of the minority experience." Historian Claudine Burnett supplies some of the context on West Long Beach.[^lb]

The brothers' Lions was Hart's Lions. C.J. Hart managed it from the mid-1960s ([The Harts](harts-santa-ana.md)), which was the brothers' era. They set no records. It was seven years in a 1941 Willys at Lions.
