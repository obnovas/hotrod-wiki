# Von Dutch: "Nothing Is Original"

!!! abstract "Article record"
    **Confidence:** Medium · **Source types:** a 1965 magazine quote (reprinted, original title unknown), a friend's memoir (Bob Burns, Letterville), LA Times exhibition story (Sept 2, 2002) · **Last verified:** 2026-10-13 (Round 7) · **Open issues:** We have not yet identified the 1965 magazine; many of the stories are secondhand · [How we rate this](../reference/source-standards.md)

## Context
Kenneth "Von Dutch" Howard (died 1992, aged 63, per the LA Times) is widely credited with reviving pinstriping in the 1950s. Most of what circulates about him is anecdote. Here we separate **his own words**, which are few, from **friends' stories**. See [Trend Books, Kits & Roth](../customs/trend-books-kits-roth.md) and his 1961 rocket car on that page.

## His own words (1965)
> "I make a point of staying right at the edge of poverty. I don't have a pair of pants without a hole in them, and the only pair of boots I have are on my feet. … There's a 'struggle' you have to go through, and if you make a lot of money it doesn't make the 'struggle' go away. It just makes it more complicated. If you keep poor, the struggle is simple."
>
> — **Von Dutch**, "a 1965 article," as reprinted by Bob Burns (Letterville). The LA Times (2002) also quotes the final two sentences.[^bb][^lat] ◐ The original magazine is not identified.

Late in life, according to the LA Times, he lettered a sign urging viewers to "use any of my stuff you want to. Nothing is original. Everything is in the subconscious, we just 'tap' it sometimes and think we have originated something." The sign was shown at Cal State Northridge in 2002.[^lat]

## A friend remembers (Bob Burns)
> "I'm a mechanic first. … If I had my way I'd be a gunsmith! I like to make things out of metal, because metal is forever. When you paint something, how long does it last? A few years, and then it's gone!"
>
> — Von Dutch, **as Burns remembered** him saying. *Secondhand recollection.*[^bb]

Burns adds that when Dutch quit striping in 1958, customers did not say what they wanted: "he just told him how much 'time' he wanted to purchase."[^bb] He says Dutch started striping cars "as a joke" at Al Titus' motorcycle shop in Lynwood ("Linwood" in his text).[^bb]

## Caveats
- ⚠ **Burns' history of pinstriping** says General Motors did the first American automobile striping in 1938. That is incorrect: coachline striping is far older. We do not adopt it.[^bb]
- The LA Times relays many colorful stories through other people, such as cat whiskers used for brushes and shooting at a visitor from Cleveland. We treat them as lore, not fact.[^lat]
