## Why patent drawings are treated as public domain
Text and drawings in U.S. patents are generally free to reproduce. The USPTO's rule at 37 CFR 1.71(d)–(e) lets an applicant include a copyright notice only in specific limited cases. None of the sheets used here carries one. ◐ This is our reading of USPTO practice, not legal advice.
