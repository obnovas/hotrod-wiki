# Reading Paths

Each story here stands on its own, but many are chapters of a bigger story. A reading path puts those chapters in order. Most paths run chronologically, and each step says why it comes next. Every story is on at least one path. When you are reading a story on a path, a **Part n of N** bar under the headline shows where you are and links to the step before and after.
