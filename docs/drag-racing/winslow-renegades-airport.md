# Renegades on the Taxiway: Winslow's Airport Drag Strip (1958–1985)

*A Route 66 railroad town, a city-council lease, and a club that raced the airport for a generation*

!!! abstract "Article record"
    **Confidence:** Medium (a museum panel, a local paper's club photo caption and Drag Strip List's digest of council and race notices agree on founding and closing years; meet-by-meet detail is thin) · **Source types:** <span class="hr-badge">Grassroots</span> Old Trails Museum / Winslow Route 66 Centennial panel (2024–26), *Painted Desert Tribune* (2011 club photo), Drag Strip List Arizona page · **Last verified:** 2026-11-05 (Round 33) · **Open issues:** ◐ early race schedule beyond Drag Strip List; ? strip length and sanctioning · [How we rate this](../reference/source-standards.md)

!!! quote "In their own words"
    "Founded in 1958, Winslow's Renegades Drag Club held events on the drag strip at the Winslow Municipal Airport. The Winslow Drag Strip hosted its last event in 1985."

    — Old Trails Museum panel, "100 Years of Route 66 in Winslow," Auto Businesses on Route 66.[^otm]

## Council permission

On March 18, 1958, the Winslow city council approved an agreement with the Winslow Renegades Drag Club to let the club use the access road and taxi strip at the municipal airport for a drag strip. Drag Strip List dates the club and the strip from that year through about 1985.[^dslaz][^otm]

A July 2011 *Painted Desert Tribune* caption on a period club photograph names the officers: Ronnie Carlson, secretary/treasurer; Johnny Pritchard, publicity (the *Tribune* caption spells it "Pritcard"); Duane Miller, strip manager; Skip Buckey, vice president; Dick Holt, vice secretary/treasurer; Ron Miller, president. The paper says the Renegades were active in the late 1950s and early 1960s and "regularly held drag racing events on the runways at Lindbergh Municipal Airport"—Winslow's airport under its Lindbergh name.[^pdt]

## Third Sundays, and a championship

On May 17, 1959, races ran on the 5,700-foot-long track; Duane Miller managed them. Meets were generally on the third Sunday of the month that year. The Northern Arizona Championship drag races came to Winslow on May 7–8, 1960.[^dslaz]<sup class="hr-q" id="qr-1"><a href="#q-1" title="Single source so far: see the bottom of the page">◐</a></sup>

## Last event, 1985

The Old Trails Museum's Route 66 centennial panel, developed with the Historic Route 66 Association of Arizona, the city and the chamber, puts the strip's last event in 1985 and notes that Winslow's Just Cruis'n Car Club formed in 1993 with some former Renegades among its founders.[^otm]<sup class="hr-q" id="qr-2"><a href="#q-2" title="Open question: see the bottom of the page">?</a></sup>
