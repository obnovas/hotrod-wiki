# Fifteen Miles East of Yuma: Wellton's Airstrip, the Barons and the Yuma Timing Association (1952–1958)

*An emergency airstrip, a Bureau of Reclamation blessing, and a March of Dimes race that packed 545 into the desert*

## Wellton, 1952

Drag races first started being held about 1952 at an abandoned World War II emergency airstrip fifteen miles east of Yuma—Wellton Auxiliary Army Airfield, by Drag Strip List's identification. The Dragsters of Yuma car club ran the racing with Bureau of Reclamation approval. By early 1953 the fastest club member had been timed at 79 mph.[^dslaz]<sup class="hr-q" id="qr-1"><a href="#q-1" title="Single source so far: see the bottom of the page">◐</a></sup>

## The Barons' strip on Highway 80

In 1956 the Barons hot rod club opened a new drag strip just south of Highway 80, east of Yuma. Newspaper articles disagreed on how far: eight miles, twelve miles, or fifteen. Later notices settled on milepost fifteen and a mile off the highway.<sup class="hr-q" id="qr-2"><a href="#q-2" title="Open question: see the bottom of the page">?</a></sup> Their first race was May 27, 1956; a September meet drew racers from Arizona and California, with spectators charged 75 cents. Races followed on October 14 and December 9, 1956, and on May 12 and June 23, 1957.[^dslaz]

Early 1957 racing was sponsored by the Tri-Valley Timing Association under NHRA sanction. About October 1957 the timing association was reorganized as the Yuma Timing Association and raced monthly. On January 12, 1958, it put on a March of Dimes benefit that drew 545 people and 72 entries; Carl Grimes of Phoenix took top eliminator and top time at 125.34 mph in his Buick-powered Fiat, and the strip donated $200. Russell Byrd Jr. was the strip manager.[^dslaz]
