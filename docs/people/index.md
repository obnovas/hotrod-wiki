# People & Builders

*The machinists, racers, publishers and families who built the sport, usually before anyone noticed.*

Most of them started in a garage, a shed or a borrowed corner of someone else's machine shop. Here they are, roughly in the order the story needs them.

*Short on time?* The [Quick Answers](../reference/quick-answers.md#people) give the short version of the big questions about the people, each with a link to the full story.

- [Ed Winfield](ed-winfield.md): cam grinder and carburetor maker; the generation before the speed-shop boom.
- [George Riley](george-riley.md): Model A/B overhead conversions, 2-port and 4-port.
- [Jimmy Summers](jimmy-summers.md): prewar Melrose Avenue customizer, fadeaway fender kits.
- [Barney Navarro](barney-navarro.md): Glendale machinist, early GMC-blown flatheads.
- [Chet Herbert](chet-herbert.md): roller cams, the "Beast" streamliners, Drag News.
- [Alex Xydias](alex-xydias.md): So-Cal Speed Shop founder, 1946.

- [Veda Orr](veda-orr.md): wartime SCTA newsletter to 750+ GIs; the Pictorials.
- [Roy Hagy](roy-hagy.md): prewar top-chopper who metal-finished his work.
- [George DuVall](george-duvall.md): So. Cal. Plating designer; the DuVall windshield and Hollywood hubcap.
- [Tom Beatty](tom-beatty.md): Glendale machinist and the record-setting belly tank.
- [Doris Herbert](doris-herbert.md): Drag News publisher, 1959–76; Standard 1320.
- [Ed Almquist](ed-almquist.md): Pennsylvania mail-order speed and the 1946 manual.
- [Peggy Hart](peggy-hart.md): racer and co-promoter at Santa Ana.
- [Julian Doty](julian-doty.md): DuVall's nephew, B&S garage, signer of SCTA timing slips.
- [Ed Iskenderian](ed-iskenderian.md): 1921–2026; the Camfather.
- [Setto Postoian](setto-postoian.md): Farmington Speed Shop; the "swamp rat" ad.

- [Stu Hilborn](stu-hilborn.md): mechanical fuel injection from the dry lakes to Indy.
- [Fran Hernandez](fran-hernandez.md): Goleta nitro, Edelbrock, Lincoln-Mercury racing and the flip-top Comets.
- [Al Bergler](al-bergler.md): Detroit tin man; first Ridler Award; Motown Shaker.
- [Malcolm Durham](malcolm-durham.md): Strip Blazer; East Coast match racing.

- [Gene Snow](gene-snow.md): Fort Worth used-car dealer; *Rambunctious*, the first 200-mph Funny Car (⚠ date).
- [Bob Bartel](bob-bartel.md): the ATAA, the World Series of Drag Racing and Quad City Drag Strip, Cordova.
- [Joe Lunati](joe-lunati.md): Memphis Rodder, Corvair and Devin racer, founder of Lunati Cams (⚠ dates).
- [Red Greth](red-greth.md): Tucson's Speed Sport roadster, fiberglass drapes, a B-29 steering wheel and a shop that mentored the neighborhood (⚠ birth town).
- [Arnie "Woo Woo" Ginsburg](arnie-ginsburg.md): the Night Train DJ whose Ginsburger ads filled Adventure Car Hop; Route 1's quarter-mile street course.
- [Don Garlits](don-garlits.md): Tampa bookkeeper to Swamp Rat; Zephyrhills 1950, the California challenge, the 1970 rear-engine redesign and the Ocala museum (⚠ frame year, roll-bar meet).
- [Don Prudhomme](don-prudhomme.md): Van Nuys painter, Greer-Black-Prudhomme, Leong's Hawaiian, Road Kings rickshaw initiation, Greer-Black-Prudhomme, Leong's Hawaiian, Snake vs. Mongoose (⚠ Schiefer record).
- [Keith Black](keith-black.md): Huntington Park driveway mechanic, backyard boat engines, Greer-Black-Prudhomme and the KB aluminum block (⚠ birth year, win count, 392 or 426).
- [Tom McEwen](tom-mcewen.md): Pensacola-born, Long Beach-raised Mongoose; Lions 1964, Hot Wheels, five NHRA nationals including Indianapolis 1978 (⚠ death date; son's name and age).
- [Gene Cromer](gene-cromer-moonlighter.md): Anderson, S.C., builder of every car he raced; the Willys gasser his wife named the *Moonlighter*, an A/Gas win at Donaldson AFB in 1965 (? whether it was the base's only meet), and racing again in his eighties (⚠ 1940 or 1941 Willys).
- [Roland Leong](roland-leong.md): "The Hawaiian": Kahuku gas dragsters, Dragmasters, the crash that made him an owner, and Prudhomme (⚠ when they met).
- Bruce Miller: see [*Street Rod* magazine](../media/street-rod-magazine-portland.md).
