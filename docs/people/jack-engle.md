## Santa Monica and the Low Flyers
Engle was born June 16, 1920, in Regina, Saskatchewan, to American parents, and grew up in Santa Monica. He went to Venice High and Santa Monica City College.[^wiki] He belonged to the **Santa Monica Low Flyers**. A family photo on Kustomrama shows him with fellow members Phil Remington, Howard Wilson and Rob McCausland. Wikipedia's list of club members also includes Stu Hilborn, Jim Travers and Frank Coon.[^kr][^wiki] In 1939 he ran 113 mph at El Mirage in a Model T roadster on Model A rails with a McCulloch-blown Cragar Model A engine (family caption, Mark Engle).[^kr]

## A homemade grinder before Isky?
- The family's caption shows Jack "grinding one of first cams on a homemade cam grinder made from a benchtop lathe and a tool post grinder... early 1941 before Isky ground his first cams." In 2018 his son Mark told Kustomrama that "he and Isky both bought their first cams from Ed Winfield."[^kr] ◐ *This is family oral history, single-sourced. The Winfield link fits the mentoring story on the [Iskenderian](ed-iskenderian.md) page.*
- **⚠ Founding-date conflict:**

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Began grinding 1939; formally opened Engle Manufacturing Co. 1941 | Company obituary flyer[^obit] |
| "Grinding cams... since 1941" | englecams.com[^site] |
| Started Engle Cams in 1942 | Kustomrama[^kr] |
| Opened an engine shop in his father's radio/TV store by 1947, bought a Landis grinder, became Engle Racing Cams in 1950 | Wikipedia[^wiki] |

  These can be reconciled as a hobby in 1939–41, a business name in 1941–42, and a full-time cam shop after the war, but that reading is ours, not the sources'.
- Wikipedia says he worked at North American Aviation and went back there after the war.[^wiki] ◐ Single-sourced.

## Reputation
Wikipedia credits his Oldsmobile cams with beating the Chryslers on the strip, which "really put him on the map."[^wiki] ◐ This needs a period source such as Drag News results. Engle was one of the three camgrinders in the [Camgrinder Wars](../eras/1950s-camgrinder-wars.md) story. He died in Santa Monica on Nov 14, 2008.[^wiki][^obit] Engle Racing Cams now operates from Long Beach.[^site]
