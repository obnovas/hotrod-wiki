# Jack Engle: The Low Flyers' Cam Grinder

*1920–2008*

!!! abstract "Article record"
    **Confidence:** Medium · **Source types:** <span class="hr-badge">Grassroots</span> family oral history, company obituary, secondary · **Last verified:** 2026-10-13 (Round 4) · **Open issues:** Founding date conflict (1939–1950) · [How we rate this](../reference/source-standards.md)

The family's photo caption is the hook: Jack Engle "grinding one of first cams on a homemade cam grinder made from a benchtop lathe and a tool post grinder... early 1941 before Isky ground his first cams."[^kr]

## Santa Monica and the Low Flyers

Engle was born June 16, 1920, in Regina, Saskatchewan, to American parents, and grew up in Santa Monica. He went to Venice High and Santa Monica City College.[^wiki] He belonged to the **Santa Monica Low Flyers**. A family photo on Kustomrama shows him with fellow members Phil Remington, Howard Wilson and Rob McCausland. Wikipedia's list of club members also includes Stu Hilborn, Jim Travers and Frank Coon.[^kr][^wiki]

He was racing before he was grinding. In 1939 he ran 113 mph at El Mirage in a Model T roadster on Model A rails with a McCulloch-blown Cragar Model A engine (family caption, Mark Engle).[^kr]

## A homemade grinder before Isky?

The family credits him with grinding cams before Isky. In 2018 his son Mark told Kustomrama that "he and Isky both bought their first cams from Ed Winfield."[^kr]<sup class="hr-q" id="qr-1"><a href="#q-1" title="Single source so far: see Open questions">◐</a></sup><sup class="hr-q" id="qr-2"><a href="#q-2" title="Sources disagree: see Open questions">⚠</a></sup>

When he started is told four different ways; the sources are compared under [Open questions](#open-questions-disputed-details).

During the war, Wikipedia says, he worked at North American Aviation and went back there after the war.[^wiki]<sup class="hr-q" id="qr-3"><a href="#q-3" title="Single source so far: see Open questions">◐</a></sup>

## Reputation

After the war the cams made his name. Wikipedia credits his Oldsmobile cams with beating the Chryslers on the strip, which "really put him on the map."[^wiki]<sup class="hr-q" id="qr-4"><a href="#q-4" title="Single source so far: see Open questions">◐</a></sup> Engle was one of the three camgrinders in the [Camgrinder Wars](../eras/1950s-camgrinder-wars.md) story.

He died in Santa Monica on Nov 14, 2008.[^wiki][^obit] Engle Racing Cams now operates from Long Beach.[^site]

## Open questions & disputed details

*From the detective's notebook: where the sources disagree, where we have only one, and what we are still looking for. Each item links back to its place in the story.*

<a id="q-1" class="hr-q-anchor"></a>**Single source so far.** **Did Engle grind cams before Isky?** This is family oral history, from his son Mark to Kustomrama.[^kr] The Winfield link fits the mentoring story on the [Iskenderian](ed-iskenderian.md) page. <a class="hr-q-back" href="#qr-1" title="Back to the story">↩</a>

<a id="q-2" class="hr-q-anchor"></a>**Disputed.** **When did the business start?** Four versions, in the table above: grinding from 1939 and Engle Manufacturing Co. from 1941 (the company's obituary flyer[^obit]); "since 1941" (englecams.com[^site]); 1942 (Kustomrama[^kr]); Engle Racing Cams in 1950 (Wikipedia[^wiki]). <a class="hr-q-back" href="#qr-2" title="Back to the story">↩</a>

<a id="q-3" class="hr-q-anchor"></a>**Single source so far.** **North American Aviation before and after the war?** Only Wikipedia says so.[^wiki] <a class="hr-q-back" href="#qr-3" title="Back to the story">↩</a>

<a id="q-4" class="hr-q-anchor"></a>**Single source so far.** **Did his Olds cams beat the Chryslers?** Only Wikipedia says so; it needs a period source such as *Drag News* results.[^wiki] <a class="hr-q-back" href="#qr-4" title="Back to the story">↩</a>

**When did he start?** The sources give four versions:

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Began grinding 1939; formally opened Engle Manufacturing Co. 1941 | Company obituary flyer[^obit] |
| "Grinding cams... since 1941" | englecams.com[^site] |
| Started Engle Cams in 1942 | Kustomrama[^kr] |
| Opened an engine shop in his father's radio/TV store by 1947, bought a Landis grinder, became Engle Racing Cams in 1950 | Wikipedia[^wiki] |

These can be reconciled as a hobby in 1939–41, a business name in 1941–42, and a full-time cam shop after the war, but that reading is ours, not the sources'.
