**Garlits at Cordova.** At Cordova in August 1957 an unknown Don Garlits learned from Emery Cook how to build a hand-operated fuel pump and beat Cook & Bedwell in the semis; Detroit's Setto Postoian beat him in the final of the ATAA World Series in 1957 and 1958, by most accounts (a 1958 newspaper preview reads as 1956 and 1957). Full story: [The Upset at Cordova](../drag-racing/detroit-cordova.md#1957-the-garlits-upset).
