**Peoria, 1970.** Hagerty, citing MotorTrend, gives August 14–16, 1970, and 600 street rods for the first Street Rod Nationals. *Hot Rod* gave the same 600 in October 1972; the figure of about 200 in Rod & Custom's account counts the drivers in the rally.
