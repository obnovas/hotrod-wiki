**The club fine rules.** Several small-town clubs made a member pay twice for a traffic ticket. Belleville's Gear Jammers had him pay the club a sum equal to half his fine; the Sedalia Drag-Ons charged one-half his court fine and put him on trial before the club; Stroudsburg's Ridge Runners took up to one-half of the fine. Escanaba's Drag-n's suspended a member for 30 days instead. No source says any club copied another. Full story: [Belleville's Gear Jammers](../drag-racing/belleville-gear-jammers.md#the-club-with-the-half-fine-rule).
